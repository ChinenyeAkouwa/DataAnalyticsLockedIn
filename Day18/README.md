Time-Based Demand Analysis

This stage of the project focuses on identifying time-based demand patterns.

Analyses conducted:

Monthly revenue trends

Demand distribution by time of day

Demand distribution by day of week

An initial challenge was that Excel sorted months alphabetically instead of chronologically. Grouping the date field correctly resolved this issue and allowed the trend to be visualized properly.

Early observations suggest that revenue fluctuations are primarily driven by changes in order volume rather than significant shifts in average order value.
