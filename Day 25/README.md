Business Problem Overview

This project analyzes operational and sales data from Nexora Technologies, a B2B enterprise software and BI solutions provider experiencing revenue instability.
Initial assumptions pointed to pricing and competition. However, exploratory data analysis revealed deeper operational patterns affecting performance.

Key Findings

• Revenue volatility driven primarily by demand fluctuations

• Heavy revenue concentration among a small product group

• Significant regional performance differences

• Strong seasonal demand cycles

The core issue identified was limited performance visibility and demand planning misalignment.
