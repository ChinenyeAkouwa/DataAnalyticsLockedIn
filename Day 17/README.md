KPI Definition and Metric Setup

In this stage of the Product Demand Analytics project, I established the key performance indicators used throughout the dashboard.

Metrics implemented:

Total Revenue

Total Units Sold

Average Order Value (AOV)

Revenue per Unit

Average Units per Order

During implementation I initially miscalculated AOV by averaging total revenue values. This was corrected to ensure it accurately reflects the typical value of a transaction.

Establishing clear KPI definitions early helps ensure that downstream visualizations and analysis remain consistent and meaningful.
